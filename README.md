Cryptocurrency Market Analysis & KPI Dashboard
Project Overview

This project analyzes cryptocurrency market data from March 16 and March 17, 2022 to understand market capitalization, trading volume, price movements, and cryptocurrency performance.

The project uses Python and Pandas for data cleaning and analysis, Excel for analytical outputs, and Looker Studio to create an interactive cryptocurrency dashboard.

Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

Analyze cryptocurrency market capitalization
Analyze 24-hour trading volume
Identify cryptocurrency gainers and losers
Analyze day-over-day price movements
Compare cryptocurrency performance
Present key insights through an interactive dashboard

The dashboard contains five analytical pages.

1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of cryptocurrency market performance.

Key components include:

Total Market Capitalization
Total 24-Hour Trading Volume
Average 24-Hour Change
Number of Gainers
Number of Losers
Top Cryptocurrencies by Market Capitalization

The market capitalization visualization compares major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, BNB, USD Coin, XRP, Terra, Solana, and Cardano, with the remaining cryptocurrencies grouped as Others.

2. Trading Volume Analysis

Analyzes cryptocurrency trading activity using 24-hour trading volume.

The page includes a comparison of the top cryptocurrencies by trading volume, with the remaining cryptocurrencies grouped as Others.

3. Price Performance Analysis

Analyzes day-over-day cryptocurrency price movements between March 16 and March 17, 2022.

The page includes:

Top 10 Cryptocurrency Gainers
Top 10 Cryptocurrency Losers
Day-over-day price change percentage

Key Insights
Bitcoin (BTC) had the highest market capitalization at approximately $776.08 billion.
Tether (USDT) recorded the highest 24-hour trading volume at approximately $44.13 billion.
Xido Finance (XIDO) recorded the highest day-over-day gain at approximately 57.74%.
bZx Protocol (BZRX) recorded the largest day-over-day decline at approximately 64.71%.
On March 17, 346 cryptocurrencies were classified as gainers, while 127 were classified as losers.

Note: This analysis represents cryptocurrency market data from March 16–17, 2022 and does not represent current cryptocurrency market conditions.
